This Privacy Policy explains what information DW Nirman Engineerings LLP may process when you interact with our website ([WEBSITE DOMAIN]) and communicate with us, primarily via WhatsApp.

Information We Collect

Because our primary contact method is WhatsApp, most information you share is provided voluntarily by you during a conversation — such as your name, phone number, project location and requirements. We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, understand your requirement, prepare indicative discussions and communicate about your project. We do not sell your information.
